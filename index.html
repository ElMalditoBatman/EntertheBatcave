🦇 ENTER THE BATCAVE

Personal Health Intelligence Dashboard

Enter the Batcave is a personal health data dashboard that integrates with Google Health API to securely aggregate and display your fitness, sleep, nutrition, and activity metrics — all from your own private infrastructure.

📊 Data Collected

  • Activity & Fitness Steps, distance, active minutes
  • Sleep Duration, stages, quality metrics
  • Nutrition Food logs, macronutrients
  • Health Metrics Heart rate, weight, vitals
  • Profile Basic user profile information
  • Settings Health platform preferences

🔒 Privacy

All data is stored locally on your own server. No third-party access, no analytics, no tracking. Your health data never leaves your control. See our Privacy Policy for details.

⚙️ How It Works

The Batcave runs on a self-hosted infrastructure using Hermes Agent, connecting to Google Health API via OAuth 2.0 with read-only access. Data is queried on-demand and displayed through your personal Discord workstation.
